Privacy Policy

Kyudo Scoreboard

Effective date: 1 October 2026

Kyudo Scoreboard is a Kyudo scoring and training application developed by Tiong Kai Hu.

Information and Data Collection

Kyudo Scoreboard does not currently collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or user data with the developer or third parties.

Information entered into the application, including archer names, scores, arrow placement, training sessions, and session history, is stored locally on the user's device and is used to provide the application's scoring, training, and history functions.

Local Storage

Kyudo Scoreboard stores application settings and session information locally on the user's device. This information is not transmitted to the developer or to an external server.

Users can delete stored sessions from within the application. Application data may also be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application, subject to the backup and restoration functionality provided by the user's device and operating system.

Data Sharing

Kyudo Scoreboard does not sell or share user data with third parties.

Advertising, Analytics and Tracking

The current version of Kyudo Scoreboard does not contain third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Device Permissions

The current Android version of Kyudo Scoreboard does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, or other sensitive Android permissions.

Data Security

Kyudo Scoreboard stores scoring and session information locally on the user's device. Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security for their device and operating system.

Children's Privacy

Kyudo Scoreboard does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The application does not currently transmit user-entered information to the developer or to external servers.

Future Features

Future versions of Kyudo Scoreboard may introduce additional functionality, such as optional cloud backup, synchronisation, user accounts, or online services. If these features change how user data is collected, used, stored, or shared, this Privacy Policy and applicable app-store disclosures will be updated accordingly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Kyudo Scoreboard's functionality or data practices change. The current version of the policy will be published on this page.
